Your spare phone should not become a second inbox.

Twinotify makes two Android phones behave like one calm notification surface. Read on either. Dismiss on either. Keep moving.

The handoff is the product.

No cloud dashboard to babysit. Twinotify chooses the best available route and shows you exactly what is happening.

Route / 01

Nearby feels instant.
Far away still arrives.

On the same Wi-Fi, your phones connect directly. When that path disappears, the encrypted relay carries the notification and holds it until the other phone is ready.

  • Direct LAN preferred automatically
  • Durable 24-hour relay mailbox
  • Clear queued and reconnecting states
Privacy / 02

The relay cannot read
the alert it carries.

Notification content is encrypted on the sending phone and authenticated on the receiving phone. The relay sees ciphertext and routing metadata, never the title or message body.

Control / 03

Mirror what matters.
Block what does not.

Choose apps one by one. Banking, password, and common one-time-code apps start protected by a hash-verified built-in denylist.

Filtering happens before encryption and delivery.

Actions / 04

Take action here.
It responds there.

Dismissals stay synchronized. Supported notification actions, including reply and mark as read, travel securely back to the source phone.

Available actions depend on the originating Android app.
