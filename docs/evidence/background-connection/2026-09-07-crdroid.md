# Background connection investigation — 2026-09-07

User reports intermittent delivery and slow network recovery while backgrounded or locked,
with battery usage set to unrestricted on both phones.

## Targets and limits

- POCO F1: Android 15. User identifies ROM as crDroid; queried crDroid version property was empty.
- Mi 11X: Android 16, crDroid version property 16.0.
- Existing installed builds: versionName 0.1.0, last updated September 6. No APK installed during this investigation.
- Both phones connected over USB and charging. This is not an unplugged overnight or CPU-suspend test.
- Installed builds do not expose the debug E2E state provider. No queue-convergence claim is made.

## Observations

- Both notification listeners and foreground SyncServices were running before and after testing, with unchanged process IDs.
- Background app-op allowed; neither package appeared in the device-idle whitelist.
- Ordinary screen locking preserved services.
- Simulated unplugged deep idle refused to advance beyond INACTIVE on both ROMs.
- Forced light idle succeeded. Both ROMs reported DOZE among policy reasons but effective network blocking NONE for the foreground app. Missing whitelist membership therefore did not establish the cause.
- Mobile data was disabled on both devices. POCO transport attempts reported Bluetooth unavailable.
- With both screens locked, disabling POCO Wi-Fi produced peer closure and failed relay DNS attempts. This was loss of connectivity, not a successful cellular handover.
- On Wi-Fi restoration, POCO logged network_changed at 11:55:25.243, immediately restarted its coordinator, and authenticated relay at 11:55:26.982 (about 1.74 seconds after the callback). LAN authenticated at 11:55:32.781.
- The Mi 11X authenticated relay after losing its LAN peer and subsequently returned to LAN.
- After recovery, one synthetic shell notification in each direction appeared on the opposite phone while screens remained locked. Checked only synthetic titles, not user notification contents. No precise end-to-end latency measurement was taken.

## Cleanup and remaining work

Wi-Fi restored to enabled on POCO; battery simulation reset and forced idle cleared on both.
No battery exemptions, cellular settings, Bluetooth settings, pairing, or app data changed.
Two synthetic test notifications were posted and may be dismissed by the user.

The reported intermittent failure was not reproduced. Need an unplugged failure on stable Wi-Fi,
including whether reopening the app immediately restores queued delivery, to distinguish CPU
suspension, transport liveness, listener capture, and service recovery. Earlier source changes
for same-network recovery callbacks have not been installed or physically verified here.
